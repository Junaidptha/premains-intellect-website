Terms of Professional Engagement

The legal and operational terms governing advisory, bookkeeping, tax preparation, and outsourcing services provided by PREMAINS INTELLECT LLP.

1. Engagement Scope & Deliverables

PREMAINS INTELLECT LLP provides financial accounting, bookkeeping, tax return preparation assistance, and virtual controller services as detailed in individual Statements of Work (SOW) or Engagement Letters executed between the Firm and the Client.

2. Division of Professional Responsibility

For CPA and Accounting firm partnerships, PREMAINS INTELLECT LLP acts as a dedicated preparation and compilation resource. The client (licensed CPA, CA, or tax agent) retains ultimate signing, e-filing, and audit representation responsibility for all filings submitted to statutory authorities (IRS, CRA, ATO, FTA), unless explicitly contracted otherwise.

3. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

Both parties agree to hold all proprietary trade secrets, financial records, client names, and work papers in strict confidence. This obligation survives termination of the engagement indefinitely.

4. Billing, Retainers & Termination

Dedicated monthly resource agreements are billed in advance on the 1st of each billing cycle with 15-day payment terms. Either party may terminate a dedicated resource agreement by providing thirty (30) days written notice. Fixed-fee projects are invoiced according to agreed milestone deliverables.

5. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These terms are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any legal dispute arising under these terms shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in New Delhi, India, with mandatory good-faith mediation preceding formal litigation.
