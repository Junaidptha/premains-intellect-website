UAE Corporate Tax Law: Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP) Rules Demystified

The UAE's 9% Federal Corporate Tax regime introduces stringent economic substance and qualifying income conditions. Discover the vital auditing checklist for Free Zone businesses to retain 0% tax benefit.

1. The New UAE Corporate Tax Reality

With the enactment of Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022, the United Arab Emirates enacted a headline 9% Federal Corporate Tax rate for taxable profits exceeding AED 375,000. While the law preserves tax incentives for entities incorporated in UAE Free Zones (such as DMCC, DIFC, JAFZA, and DAFZA), 0% tax treatment is no longer automatic.

To enjoy a 0% corporate tax rate on qualifying income, an entity must establish and maintain its status as a Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP). Failing even a single statutory test disqualifies the business, subjecting all net profit to the standard 9% tax rate for a minimum lock-in period of five tax years.

2. The Four Mandatory QFZP Tests

Under Cabinet Decision No. 55 of 2023 and Ministerial Decision No. 139 of 2023, a Free Zone company must satisfy all of the following criteria:

  • Adequate Economic Substance: Maintain sufficient qualified staff, incur adequate operating expenditure, and conduct core income-generating activities (CIGA) within the Free Zone.
  • Derive "Qualifying Income": Transactions with other Free Zone persons, or specific qualifying activities with non-Free Zone persons (e.g., manufacturing, treasury, logistics, fund management).
  • Comply with Transfer Pricing Rules: Transactions with domestic or international related parties must adhere to arm's-length principles with documented benchmark files.
  • Prepare Audited IFRS Financial Statements: The company must maintain formal audited financial statements prepared in accordance with IFRS.

3. The De Minimis Rule: A Critical Safety Valve

If a Free Zone entity derives non-qualifying revenue (such as commercial transactions with UAE Mainland individuals or excluded activities), it can still maintain its QFZP status provided its non-qualifying revenue does not exceed the De Minimis threshold: the lower of AED 5,000,000 or 5% of total revenue.
