Privacy Policy & Client Data Protection

Last updated: October 2026. How PREMAINS INTELLECT LLP collects, safeguards, and handles corporate financial information.

1. Introduction & Overview

PREMAINS INTELLECT LLP ("Firm", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to respecting your privacy and safeguarding the financial, corporate, and personal data entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy details our operational protocols under Indian Information Technology Acts, the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and international privacy standards.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to perform professional accounting, taxation, and corporate advisory services:

  • Contact information: Name, business email, telephone number, corporate address, jurisdiction.
  • Financial records: General ledgers, trial balances, bank statements, receipts, and tax filings supplied for advisory review.
  • Technical analytics: Anonymized IP addresses, browser types, and session timings captured with your cookie consent.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to deliver contracted advisory services, communicate project status, invoice for completed work, and satisfy statutory record-keeping mandates. We never sell, rent, or monetize client data under any circumstances.

4. Data Storage & Air-Gapped Security

Client financial files are processed on encrypted corporate servers and secure cloud terminals. Our staff are prohibited from downloading client records to local non-encrypted drives. USB ports are hardware-disabled, and biometric security restricts unauthorized entry to all delivery floors.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Under GDPR and applicable international laws, you possess the right to access your stored data, request corrections, request erasure of non-statutory records, or revoke consent. To exercise any data rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at chandanmallb@gmail.com.
