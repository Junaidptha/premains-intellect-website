Zero Trust Architecture

Enterprise Data Security & Client Confidentiality

Financial and tax data demands the most stringent legal and cyber protections. Here is how Premains Intellect LLP protects your client ledgers.

Legal & Contractual Safeguards

Before any file exchange or credential sharing, we execute comprehensive Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) drafted under strict international jurisdiction clauses. All team members undergo rigorous legal vetting and sign personal non-disclosure covenants enforceable under Indian and international law.

  • Mutual confidentiality agreements with strict indemnification clauses.
  • Non-solicitation protection for CPA clients and end-customers.
  • Police background verification for all accounting personnel.

Cyber & Network Security Controls

Our IT network operates under strict Zero Trust parameters. Data remains in the client's cloud software (QuickBooks, Xero, Drake); our staff interact via secure browser sessions without downloading sensitive client documents onto local hard drives.

  • Hardware USB port blocking and optical drive disabling on all workstations.
  • Static whitelisted corporate IP addresses for access authorization.
  • Encrypted BitLocker SSD drives and enterprise endpoint detection (EDR).
