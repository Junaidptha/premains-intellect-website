Headquartered in Kolkata, India, we partner with Chartered Accountancy firms, accounting practices, business owners and global corporations to provide dedicated offshore accounting, bookkeeping, taxation and financial management teams.

Our multidisciplinary team comprises Chartered Accountants (CAs), tax professionals, legal experts and financial analysts with expertise in Indian and international financial reporting frameworks, including Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS), US GAAP, IFRS and Australian Accounting Standards (AASB), along with relevant taxation and regulatory compliance requirements.

We combine advanced AI-assisted accounting and reconciliation workflows with a robust two-tier human review mechanism to enhance accuracy, maintain quality and ensure operational efficiency.
